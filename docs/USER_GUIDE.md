# User Guide

Open **https://energize-the-future.vercel.app** (or http://localhost:5173 when running locally).

> **First visit tip:** the server sleeps when nobody uses it. If the game or facts take a
> little while to load the very first time, wait up to a minute — then everything is fast.

## Getting around

- **Language** — the globe button (top right) switches between English, Română and Русский
  instantly. Your choice is remembered.
- **Menu** — the ☰ button next to the language button lists every page. Press `Esc` or click
  outside to close it. On a keyboard, the first <kbd>Tab</kbd> reveals a *Skip to main content* link.
- **Logo** — click it to return home.

## Pages

### Home
The big picture: why SDG 7 matters, the three objectives, a few global statistics, the four main
energy sources, and six things you can do today. Two buttons lead straight to the game and the
calculator.

### About SDG 7
Short, student-friendly sections (the UN, SDG 7, energy poverty, fossil fuels, climate,
efficiency, who can help), a timeline of renewable energy from 1839 to today, and the official
SDG 7 targets (7.1, 7.2, 7.3, 7.a, 7.b).

### Renewable Energy
Click a source (solar, wind, hydropower, geothermal, biomass, tidal) to open it: how it works,
advantages, disadvantages, environmental impact, real uses and a surprising fact. The table at the
bottom compares them side by side.

### Energy-Saving Tips
20 tips grouped by home, school, transport, technology and public spaces. Each shows its
difficulty and the potential saving. Press **Mark as completed** to tick a tip — the progress
ring fills up and is saved in your browser. **Reset progress** starts over.

### Fun Facts
Browse 35 facts. Filter by category, press **Random Fact** or **Next Fact**, click the heart to
save favorites (see them with **My Favorites**), or **Share** (uses your device's share sheet, or
copies the text).

### Mini Game — Energy Hero
You are the energy advisor of Greenville, a coal-powered city.

1. Type your name (optional) and press **Start Game**.
2. On each of the 10 levels read the situation, pick one of two options and press **Confirm Choice**.
   - Each option shows its **cost**. Your **budget** is limited (you earn +20 per level), so an
     option you can't afford is locked — you can't have everything, so prioritise.
   - You have **25 seconds**. If time runs out, the cheapest option is chosen for you.
3. A **consequences** panel shows exactly how your choice changed clean energy, pollution,
   affordability, happiness, sustainability and budget (green = good, red = bad). Press
   **Next Level** to continue.
4. At the end you see your score, your **achievements** and can **Submit to Leaderboard**.
   The leaderboard is also visible on the start screen.

Score tip: sustainability is worth the most points, so long-term choices pay off.

### Energy Calculator
1. Enter an appliance (name, power in watts, hours per day, days per month, price per kWh) and
   press **Add Appliance**. The power is on the appliance label; the price is on your electricity bill.
2. Each appliance shows daily and monthly energy, monthly and yearly cost and CO₂.
   Use the pencil to **edit** or the bin to **remove**. Everything is saved in your browser.
3. **Your Totals** adds everything up and shows how many trees would be needed to absorb the CO₂
   in a year.
4. Change the **currency** (USD, EUR, RON, MDL) any time.
5. **Compare efficient alternatives** shows how much an efficient bulb, fridge, TV or air
   conditioner saves compared with an older one, using your price. One click adds the efficient
   version to your list.

CO₂ values are estimates: they depend on how your country produces electricity.

### Community
Send an idea for a more sustainable community: name, e-mail, category and message (10–2000
characters). If something is wrong you'll see exactly what to fix; on success you get a
confirmation.

### Contact
Questions or feedback: name, e-mail, subject and message.

### AI & Credits
How the project was made, what AI helped with, where the information comes from, the open-source
software used and how privacy is handled.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| "Something went wrong" with a *Try again* button | The server may be waking up — wait ~30 s and press *Try again* |
| Leaderboard says no scores | It's empty (or the server restarted) — be the first! |
| Text is in the wrong language | Use the globe button; the choice is saved per browser |
| Progress or appliances disappeared | They live in this browser; clearing site data or using another browser/private window resets them |
