# Prompt 2 — written evaluation and the request that followed

**Author's request (11 November 2026):** *"update the project to get the best grade possible"* — sent together with
seven screenshots of an e-mailed evaluation (Submission #1, **62 % / 6.2 of 10**, "approved as an MVP foundation").

**Summary of the evaluation (paraphrased; the screenshots are not stored in the repository):**

| Criterion | Score | Main points |
|---|---|---|
| Practical and economic value | 12 / 20 | Useful calculator; needs a concrete economic benefit analysis for a real Moldovan household or school, with real tariffs |
| Scientific novelty and hypothesis | 6 / 15 | No testable scientific hypothesis |
| Technical depth and methodology | 10 / 20 | Clean architecture and tests, but the core maths is too simple for grade 12; needs 2–3 non-trivial algorithms benchmarked over at least 100 runs |
| Product quality and design | 14 / 15 | Strong; the free-tier cold start can hurt a live demo |
| Responsible AI and compliance | 9 / 10 | Three human-verification checkboxes still open (RO/RU proofreading, author can explain the code, real phone/laptop) |
| Documentation and research log | 5 / 10 | Missing 2-page abstract, 1-page visual annex, dated laboratory logbook, team-role confirmation |
| Stand design and defence | 6 / 10 | Poster should be A0 (or 2 × A1) and show hypothesis, architecture, benchmarks and economic ROI; pitch should be 3 minutes + 7 minutes of Q&A in English and Romanian |

**How it was handled:** see [AI_USAGE.md §9](../../AI_USAGE.md), [docs/research/](../research/README.md) and the
[logbook](../research/LOGBOOK.md). Items that only the author can complete (human proofreading, explaining the code,
real-device test, mentor confirmation about the solo author, official templates) are listed in
[docs/research/README.md](../research/README.md) and were **not** ticked on the author's behalf.
