Research plan — “Energize the Future”: cost-optimal appliance scheduling under a two-zone tariff

Stefania Tabarcea · Orizont, Durlești · grade [grade] · mentor: Azamat Jauysh · category III (algorithms, software applications)

1. Question and goal

Can a computer choose start times for a household’s appliances so that the electricity bill falls under Moldova’s two-zone tariff without exceeding the main fuse, and which algorithm does this best? Goal: measure the saving, compare four methods fairly, and deliver a free, multilingual tool (EN/RO/RU) that anyone can use in a browser.

2. Hypotheses (fixed before the full benchmark)

  • H1 optimised schedules cost less than habit · H2 annealing and genetic search beat greedy on a tight fuse · H3 the saving is capped by (1 − night/day price) · H4 exact search stops scaling while heuristics keep working.

3. Methods and materials

  • Model: 24 hourly prices, appliances (power, duration, allowed window, habit), fuse limit; fitness = cost + penalty × overload.
  • Solvers: habit baseline, greedy, simulated annealing, genetic algorithm, exact branch and bound; equal budget of 2400 evaluations.
  • Experiments: 200 + 193 seeded random households (two fuse regimes), scalability (6–40 appliances, 100 each), tariff sensitivity, stability over 100 seeds. Paired tests, 95% confidence intervals.
  • Data: Premier Energy official prices (3.75 / 2.94 lei/kWh from 2 April 2026), BNM exchange rate, public price listings; no personal data.
  • Tools: JavaScript, React/Vite, Node.js; 52 automated tests; Lighthouse audits; Claude Code as the only AI tool, under the author’s direction.

5. Expected results (and what was found)

Expected: a saving bounded by the night discount (22%). Found: 10.4% (5.5 kW fuse) and 5.6% (4.0 kW), about 27 EUR (539 lei) a year for a reference household; annealing 0.41% from the optimum versus 1.14% for greedy; exact search proving optimality in only 8% of 40-appliance cases.

6. Risks and ethics

  • Synthetic households and a tariff whose VAT basis is not stated: shown as limits; the tool accepts the user’s own prices.
  • No claim of CO2 savings; commercial prices are labelled indicative.
  • The tool runs in the browser and stores no personal data; AI use and mistakes are documented (AI_USAGE.md, LOGBOOK.md); sources and certainty levels are in ECONOMICS.md and FACT_CHECK.md.
